# Playroma Integration

## Playroma Sound

As of Phase 18, Playroma Sound is part of Roentgen. Its platform-neutral projects live under `src/Playroma.Sound.*`, are included in `Roentgen.sln`, and are covered by the Roentgen source manifest. No clone, submodule, source path, NuGet package, NAudio assembly, or WinForms assembly is required.

## Playroma Music

Playroma Music remains an optional pinned source dependency until Phase 19. Its future synthesis backend will target the integrated Playroma Sound projects.
