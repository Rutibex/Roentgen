# Adaptive cues, stems and transitions

The director maps game context to musical situations such as exploration, interior exploration, settlement, suspicion, stealth, combat, convergence, revelation, tragedy, victory and ending.

Situation changes are quantized to immediate, beat, bar, phrase, loop or explicit markers. A transition plan records source, destination, bridge family, quantization, requested tick, crossfade time and rationale. The bridge planner chooses crossfade, harmonic pivot, acceleration, deceleration or deliberate rupture according to key, scale, tempo and campaign significance.

Stem changes are preferred over full cue replacement for ordinary intensity changes. Dialogue reduces percussion and melody, Roentgen Focus suppresses aggressive stems while retaining diagnostic identity, and combat raises pulse, percussion and danger stems.
