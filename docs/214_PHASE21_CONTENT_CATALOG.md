# Phase 21 Content Catalog Guide

The launch pack is grouped by gameplay authority rather than by file size. Region and settlement definitions bind geography, strategic resources, services, structures, ambience, and music. Actor definitions bind role, habitat, senses, equipment, morale, target parts, injury consequences, audio, and threat music. Item definitions bind procedural assets, components, ammunition or power, Focus actions, repair, crafting, and sound. Narrative definitions bind compatible regions and factions, semantic roles, hidden facts, multiple solution routes, failures, recovery, consequences, and follow-ups.

Content IDs are public persistence contracts. Rename by deprecation and migration, never by silent replacement. Display names and descriptions may change without changing identity. A new decorative option must not reorder unrelated deterministic streams.
