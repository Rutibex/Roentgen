# Originality and Legal Separation Policy

This is a design-development policy, not legal advice.

## 1. Objective

Roentgen should capture the appeal of systemic post-apocalyptic CRPGs without presenting itself as a clone, sequel, adaptation, or substitute for a specific protected franchise.

## 2. Allowed inspiration categories

- Post-apocalyptic fiction.
- First-person CRPGs.
- Tactical pause systems.
- Body-part damage.
- Retro science fiction.
- Procedural generation.
- Immersive-sim problem solving.
- Tabletop attribute, skill, and perk systems.

These are broad concepts and genres. Implementation must still be independently authored.

## 3. Prohibited copying

Do not copy or closely paraphrase:

- Quest text.
- Dialogue.
- Lore.
- Character biographies.
- Faction structures and iconography.
- Distinctive creature designs.
- Weapon designs strongly identified with another franchise.
- UI layout and visual metaphor.
- Perk names and descriptions.
- Attribute acronyms.
- Marketing language suggesting official connection.
- Logos, mascots, badges, or symbols.

## 4. Tactical targeting separation

Roentgen Focus differs by:

- Being centered on radiographic diagnosis.
- Showing armor layers, organs, structural stress, and machine components.
- Using the original Focus resource and perk tree.
- Supporting authored target-part graphs for creatures and machinery.
- Presenting predicted injuries and systemic consequences.
- Using an original interface and terminology.

Development documents may refer to genre comparisons internally, but shipping text must use canonical Roentgen terminology.

## 5. Character-system separation

The game may use seven broad attributes and skill values, but must not:

- Use another game’s attribute acronym.
- Copy its exact derived-stat formulas.
- Copy its perk catalog.
- Copy its trait descriptions.
- Copy its item and consumable naming.

## 6. Asset policy

All imported third-party necessities require:

- Source record.
- License record.
- Attribution requirement.
- Redistribution confirmation.
- Modification permission where needed.
- Build inclusion reason.

Generated assets must not be trained or parameterized to imitate a living artist or a specific protected game’s exact style.

## 7. Naming review

Before content lock, run a naming review for:

- Terms confusingly similar to major post-apocalyptic franchises.
- Accidental use of protected organization or product names.
- Generic names that become misleading in context.
- Store text that overstates similarity.

## 8. Marketing rule

Public description should say what Roentgen is, not define it solely as “Fallout but procedural.” Comparisons may be used informally during development but should not become the product identity.
