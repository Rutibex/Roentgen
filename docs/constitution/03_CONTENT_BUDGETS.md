## 4. Solution coverage

Across major and substantial side quests, the content library must expose repeated meaningful use of:

- Combat.
- Stealth.
- Ballistics.
- Directed Energy.
- Demolitions.
- Security.
- Engineering.
- Science.
- Medicine.
- Fieldcraft.
- Influence.
- Commerce.
- Faction leverage.
- Companion abilities.
- Evidence and exploration.

No skill should exist primarily for flavor text.

## 6. Audio coverage

A launch semantic catalog must cover:

- All weapon fire, mechanisms, reloads, failures, and impacts.
- Every damage material family.
- Every locomotion surface and gait family.
- Every door, container, lock, terminal, and crafting station family.
- Every creature family’s calls, movement, attacks, injuries, and death states.
- Every robot family’s movement, weapons, damage, and failure states.
- Weather and environmental layers for all six regions.
- Settlement, ruin, underground, industrial, medical, and wilderness ambience.
- UI, inventory, map, journal, perks, crafting, trade, and Roentgen Focus feedback.
- Quest stingers and discovery cues.

## 7. Music coverage

- Six regional exploration families.
- Settlement safe and uneasy states.
- Ruin exploration.
- Underground exploration.
- Medical horror.
- Suspicion.
- Stealth tension.
- Low, medium, and high combat.
- Elite or boss combat.
- Discovery.
- Revelation.
- Tragedy.
- Faction victory and defeat.
- Companion crisis.
- Ending and epilogue.

## 8. Repetition limits

Batch analysis should flag:

- Same structure silhouette repeated within nearby visual range beyond configured tolerance.
- Same quest kernel appearing more than twice in one campaign unless transformed by substantially different bindings and outcomes.
- Same NPC name within a campaign.
- Same faction motif assigned to unrelated major factions.
- Same weapon recipe producing statistically dominant results across excessive seeds.
- Dialogue phrase repetition above style-profile thresholds.
